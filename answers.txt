sort1 uses: Bubble Sort

How do you know?: I timed sort1 and sort3 for reversed10000 after I figured out sort2 is Merge Sort.
Bubble sort is going to continue sorting the list and making n swaps in each of the n iterations as the list is reversed.
But selection sort will find the smallest element in the unsorted list and swap it with the first element of the unsorted list.
Thus, in every iteration, the unsorted list will reduce 1. So, selection sort will have a running time less than n^2. Hence, selection sort
will take slightly less time than bubble sort. Hence, as sort1 had 0.272s and sort2 had 0.177s, I concluded sort1 is Bubble Sort and sort2
is Selection Sort.
